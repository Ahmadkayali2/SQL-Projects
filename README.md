Project Introduction

This project analyzes student mental health data to evaluate whether international students experience higher risks of depression and to assess the impact of length of stay. Using PostgreSQL, I conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) on key variables, including depression scores (PHQ-9), social connectedness (SCS), and acculturative stress (ASISS).
I applied SQL techniques such as filtering, aggregation, and grouping to compare international and domestic students, and to identify relationships between mental health indicators and duration of stay. The analysis focuses on detecting patterns, correlations, and potential predictors of depression.
This project demonstrates proficiency in PostgreSQL, data querying, and analytical reasoning, as well as the ability to derive insights from structured datasets.
